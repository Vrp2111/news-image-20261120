One image summing up the world's news of **Friday 20 November 2026** — a
newspaper front page with 38 stories from 6 continents. The front page
leads with Cyclone Akvan making landfall on the Odisha coast (370,000
people evacuated), the COP31 draft dropping every mention of fossil
fuels, Asia's markets plunging as the AI rout deepens (Nikkei −4.1%,
KOSPI −4.7%, Taiwan −6.2%), GTA VI's day-one sales now estimated at
$2.36bn, and a UK–EU youth-mobility deal of 100,000 places a year.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) delivers `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. Before copying, it checks the image against the
competition gate — at most 1 MiB, exactly 1024×1024 pixels — and against
the approved image's SHA-256 hash, then reads the output back and proves
it matches byte for byte.
