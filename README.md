One image summing up the world's news of **Friday 20 November 2026** — a
newspaper front page: 39 stories across 6 continents, led by Cyclone Akvan
making landfall on the Odisha coast (370,000 evacuated), COP31 entering its
final day in Antalya with no deal in sight, and Russia pounding Kyiv with
ballistic missiles overnight.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
and validates it against the gate: at most 1 MiB, exactly 1024×1024 pixels,
and matching the approved image's SHA-256 hash.
