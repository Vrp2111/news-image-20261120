One image summing up the world's news of **Friday 20 November 2026** — a
newspaper front page: 38 stories across 6 continents, led by Cyclone Akvan
making landfall on the Odisha coast (370,000 evacuated), the COP31 draft
dropping every mention of fossil fuels, Asia's markets plunging as the AI
rout deepens, and GTA VI's day-one sales now put at $2.36bn (est.).

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
and validates it against the gate first: at most 1 MiB, exactly 1024×1024
pixels, and matching the approved image's SHA-256 hash.
